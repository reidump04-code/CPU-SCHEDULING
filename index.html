FCFS

First-Come, First-Served

Non-preemptive. Processes execute in arrival order.

SJF

Shortest Job First

Non-preemptive. Chooses the available process with the shortest burst.

SRTF

Shortest Remaining Time First

Preemptive SJF. The shortest remaining burst gets the CPU.

RR

Round Robin

Preemptive. Each process receives the selected time quantum.

Priority

Priority Scheduling

Both non-preemptive and preemptive modes are included. Smaller number means higher priority.
